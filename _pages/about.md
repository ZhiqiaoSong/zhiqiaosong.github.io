Zhiqiao Song 🌻
======
Welcome! I'm Zhiqiao 👋 Thanks for visiting me academic website.

## About me

I am a Lecturer in Actuarial Science in the Department of Mathematics at the Bay Campus, Swansea University.

I obtained my Ph.D. in the Department of Statstics and Actuarial Science at the [University of Waterloo](https://uwaterloo.ca/statistics-and-actuarial-science/), specializing in Actuarial Science and Quantitative Finance. 
I am very fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Jun Cai](https://sas.uwaterloo.ca/~jcai/). 
<!-- My current research focuses on distributional model uncertainty with applications to finance and insurance.-->

I obtained my master's degree in Financial Technology (FinTech) from [Imperial College London (IC)](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/), United Kingdom, where I worked on the diversification effect, heavy-tailedness, and risk management.
